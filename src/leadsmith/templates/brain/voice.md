## Sequence
- Step 1 (day 0): one observation about them, then 3 ideas made for them (A/B/C), then "which one first?". No link. Under 110 words.
- Step 2 (day 3): make the strongest idea tangible in 2-3 sentences; the others still stand. Under 80 words.
- Step 3 (day 7): short, easy out: offer to start with the smallest idea. Under 45 words.

## Extras
- Offer ideas: yes (email 1 offers 3 ideas made for them, "A) … B) … C)"; they reply with the one they want)
- LinkedIn connection note too: no
- 4-page PDF deck per account: no (yes = the writer builds one; you choose per email whether to attach it)

## Always
- Open with something specific and true about them (from research, with a source).
- One idea per email. One question per email.
- Plain text. Lowercase or sentence-case subjects, 2-5 words.

## Never
- Invent facts, numbers, customers, mutual connections or "saw your post".
